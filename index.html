A little context

I like useful things with a point of view.

I work at the intersection of sales, partners, technology, learning, and storytelling. My favorite projects take something complicated—an AI workflow, a partner motion, a sales methodology, or a high-stakes decision—and turn it into something people can actually use.

That might look like a certification path, an onboarding engine, a manager coaching system, a scenario roleplay, an SKO song, or a video that finally makes the idea click.
